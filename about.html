Our Story

Three secondhand shelves, then a whole storefront.

We opened in 2011 with a lease we could barely afford and a conviction that Millbrook needed a place to browse, not just buy. Fifteen years later, that's still the whole plan.

Why We're Still Here

We measure success in "you have to read this," not units sold.

Every book on our shelves has been opened by someone who works here. That's not a slogan — it's a hiring requirement. We'd rather stock 15,000 titles we can vouch for than 50,000 we can't.

When you can't find something, we don't shrug — we ask, we search our dealer network, and more often than not, we find it. That started as a favor for a regular in 2013 and turned into the special-order service that's now half of what we do.

Fifteen Years, Roughly

How we got from three shelves to this.

2011

Opened in a 600 sq ft storefront

Three secondhand shelves, a card table for a register, and a lot of borrowed folding chairs for the opening-night reading.

2014

The staff-picks shelf is born

What started as a single handwritten card taped to a shelf edge is now the first thing regulars check on every visit.

2017

Monthly book club nights begin

Six people around a folding table talking about a mystery novel. It's grown, but the folding table is still technically in service.

2020

"Ask Us to Find a Book" goes official

What used to be an informal favor became a real service, with a form, a process, and a genuine 9-day average turnaround.

2023

Expanded next door

Knocked through the wall to the old shoe-repair shop and finally gave the children's section a proper reading nook.

2026

15,000+ titles, 40+ events a year

Still four booksellers, still arguing about the staff-picks shelf, still open seven days a week.
