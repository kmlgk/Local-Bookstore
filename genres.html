Browsing Questions

How the shelves actually work.

By genre first, then alphabetically by author within each section — the same order you'd find walking the aisles. Staff Picks and New Arrivals get their own front-of-store tables regardless of genre.

We pick the section a browser is most likely to check first, and cross-reference it on our shelf-talker cards. If you're not sure where something lives, the search bar on the Books page checks every section at once.

The core twelve stay put, but the featured end-caps rotate — cookbooks grow every November, and the poetry table doubles in size for National Poetry Month each April.

We've added two this way — Graphic Novels started as a single shared shelf with Sci-Fi before enough requests split it out. Tell any bookseller at the register, or mention it on the request form.
