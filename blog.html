This Month's Contributor

Sam reads three mysteries a week and still gets surprised.

Sam's been our mystery and genre buyer since 2019 — the guy who can tell you within a chapter whether a thriller is going to stick the landing. Most of the "Genre Guide" articles on this journal started as something he said out loud at the request desk that a customer told him he should write down.

Off the clock, he's rereading the same battered copy of a locked-room mystery he won't name, "because once you've read it, you can't unread it, and I'm not doing that to you yet."
